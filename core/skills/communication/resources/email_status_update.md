# Email Status Update Template

> For stakeholders. Plain language, no fleet jargon, no emojis.

```text
Subject: [Project] update — [Date] — [On track | At risk | Blocked]

Hi [Name],

[Headline: the one sentence that matters this period.]

Completed
- [Outcome in plain language] ([link])
- [Outcome in plain language] ([link])

Coming up
- [What ships next and roughly when]

Needs your attention
- [Nothing this period. | Decision/action needed, by whom, by when.]

Overall: [x of y] planned items done. [One-line risk note if "At risk".]

Regards,
[Sender]
```
